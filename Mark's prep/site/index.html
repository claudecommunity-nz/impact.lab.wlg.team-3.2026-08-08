    Known limits
    • Thresholds were tuned by eye against this one event. One event is not a validation set.
    • The mode-mix test clears Good Friday, Easter Monday and ANZAC Day observed, but wrongly flags Easter Sunday, which had no rain.
    • Counts are hourly, so nothing can be detected sooner than the end of the hour it happens in.
    • Sensors cover instrumented streets only. A sensor knocked out by the storm looks the same as a street nobody used.
    • The timeline comes from news reporting, not a Council incident log. Times are approximate except the declaration.
    • 19 of 51 listed rainfall gauges were reporting during the window.
